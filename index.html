📖 How to Use SentinelPost AI
Step-by-step guide
1
💼 Connect Your LinkedIn
Click Connect LinkedIn at the top of the page. You'll be taken to LinkedIn to authorize the app. Once approved, you're brought back automatically and your name appears in the connected bar.
💡 Your LinkedIn title appears in the post preview. Click the ✏️ pencil icon in the preview card to set or update it anytime.
2
🔍 Search for Articles
In the Set Your Search Topic section, enter a website (like ibm.com or microsoft.com) and a topic (like AI or cybersecurity). Click 🔍 Search Articles — results appear in a few seconds.
ibm.com → AI articles
microsoft.com → Copilot
aws.amazon.com → Cloud
Any website → Any topic
💡 Use the 🔄 button next to Search to force a fresh search and get the very latest articles.
3
📰 Select an Article
Click any article from the results to select it. A blue checkmark ✓ appears on your chosen article. If you see an orange 🚨 Breaking News banner at the top, click an article there to post about something timely and urgent.
4
🎨 Choose Your Tone & Schedule
Pick a writing tone that matches how you want to come across. The app automatically highlights your best performing tone based on your past LinkedIn engagement.
Thought Leader — Insightful and authoritative. Great for establishing expertise.
Conversational — Friendly and approachable. Great for driving comments.
Technical — Deep-dive and expert. Great for technical audiences.
Inspirational — Motivating and forward-looking. Great for broad reach.
Also select your preferred day of the week to post — this is a reminder for your own scheduling habit.
5
🚀 Generate Your Post
Click 🚀 Generate LinkedIn Post. Claude AI writes a professional post tailored to your chosen article and tone in about 3 to 5 seconds. A realistic LinkedIn preview card shows exactly how your post will look — including your name, title, and the full text.
💡 Not happy with the result? Click 🔄 Generate Again to get a completely fresh version, or try a different tone.
6
📤 Publish to LinkedIn
Click 📤 Post to LinkedIn to publish directly to your profile with one click — no copy and paste needed. The post goes live immediately. Alternatively, click 📋 Copy Post to paste it into LinkedIn yourself.
✅ Your LinkedIn connection expires every 60 days. Just click Connect LinkedIn again when prompted to re-authorize.
✨ What Happens Automatically
🚨
Breaking News Alerts — IBM's newsroom is checked every morning. If a major announcement is detected, an orange banner appears at the top of the app with articles ready to post about.
📊
Engagement Intelligence — After you publish posts, your LinkedIn likes and comments are tracked. Over time, the app learns which tone your audience responds to best and highlights it automatically.
🔄
Weekly Article Refresh — Every Monday at 8am fresh articles are fetched automatically. Your content is always current without any action on your part.
